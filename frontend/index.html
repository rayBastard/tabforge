TabForge

A song comes in as sound — and leaves as tablature.

Drop any track — TabForge splits it into instruments (guitar, bass, keys, vocals, drums), transcribes the ones you pick into notes and tablature, and opens the result as a playable, editable score. Everything runs on your machine.
